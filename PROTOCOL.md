# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18 after confirming coverage and source units. Model one USD notional funded in yen, settling in dollars. Reset the FX and funding positions at each available New York H.10 observation, keeping USD notional fixed while P&L accumulates in the margin account. Initial margin is 1/leverage. Liquidate after marking at an observed spot if equity reaches 40% of initial margin. Use a 1 bp opening and exit cost, Act/365 simple rate accrual across weekend/holiday gaps, and annual funding spread scenarios of 0, 50 and 150 bp. Rate proxies are DFF with a two-calendar-day information lag and the Japan interbank monthly rate with a two-month lag from the start of the accrual. These are lag assumptions applied to revised downloaded series.

Fit on 2004–2021 end dates. Reserve 2022–2024 for descriptive non-overlapping fixed-horizon replay and August 1–7, 2024 for the stress case. Each path runs 21 consecutive H.10 observation intervals. Compare independent Gaussian draws of daily log FX moves with moving-block sampling of log moves. Use identical accrued interest and calendar-gap schedules for both simulators, sampled in contiguous training blocks independently of FX draws. Use 4,000 replications, fixed seeds, leverage 3, 8, 15 and 25, base block length 5 with lengths 2 and 10 as robustness. Report 95th-percentile peak-to-trough equity drawdown divided by initial margin, fifth-percentile closeout return, breach fraction and binomial Monte Carlo standard error. Compare training draw generators over identical parameters. Record annual fee and daily amount separately from the USD interaction from converting yen interest. Check all accounting with the Aug. 1–5 path and a synthetic fixture. No causal claims or historical forecast inference.
